# Design Requirements

- shots missed instead of shots made
- Starting position in auton
- use preloaded value in pre-match to create a base number of balls attempted and use that value for shots attempted
- Pickup Location (depot, outpost, midfield) and one of the 3 outcomes
- 3 outcomes —> Successful, Attempted, Didn’t do
- Midfield Feeding boolean for auton and teleop
- keep track of 5 starting positions and boolean of if they drove or not
- teleop activity (given all 6 phases and what they did
    - traack pickup (alliance zone, outpost, neutral zone, opponent area) 
    - defense
    - when they shot
- endgame activity
    - attempting pickup, shooting, or defense
- did they prefer bump or trench? —> in post-game
